# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** January 1, 2026

## Data we collect

We collect account email, usage telemetry, and probe run metadata (target URLs, scores, timestamps).
We do **not** collect end-user conversations from your production applications unless you explicitly enable logging.

## Retention

- Account data: retained while the account is active plus **90 days** after deletion request.
- Probe run artifacts: retained for **365 days** on Pro plans, **30 days** on Free.
- Audit logs: **7 years** for enterprise contracts.

## Third parties

We use subprocessors for hosting (cloud provider), error tracking, and payment processing.
A current list is published at `/legal/subprocessors`.

## Your rights

GDPR and CCPA rights include access, correction, deletion, and portability.
Submit requests to **privacy@agentprobe.example**.

## International transfers

EU customer data is processed in **EU regions** by default.
Cross-border transfers use Standard Contractual Clauses where required.
