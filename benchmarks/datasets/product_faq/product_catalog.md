# Product Catalog FAQ

## Electronics

All electronics include a **1-year manufacturer warranty**.
Extended warranty may be purchased within **30 days** of delivery.

Warranty excludes accidental damage, water damage, and unauthorized modifications.

## Apparel

Apparel items may be returned within **30 days** if unworn with tags attached.
Final-sale clearance items are marked **non-returnable** in checkout.

## Subscriptions

Software subscriptions renew monthly unless cancelled **48 hours** before the renewal date.
Downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle.
