# Shipping & Returns FAQ

## Shipping times

- **Standard shipping:** 5–7 business days ($5.99)
- **Express shipping:** 2–3 business days ($14.99)
- **International:** 10–14 business days (duties may apply)

Free standard shipping applies to orders **over $50** in the contiguous United States.

## Tracking

Tracking numbers are emailed within **24 hours** of shipment.
Use the tracking link in your order confirmation email.

## Returns

- **Full refund** within **30 days** of delivery for unused items in original packaging.
- **50% refund** available from day 31 through day **90** for eligible categories.
- **No refunds** after 90 days except where required by law.
- Digital downloads are **non-refundable** once accessed.

## Exchanges

Exchanges are processed as a return plus a new order.
Contact **support@example.com** with order ID **ORD-** prefix.

## Damaged items

Report damage within **7 days** of delivery with photos for a prepaid return label.
